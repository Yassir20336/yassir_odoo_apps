Multi-Company Stock Details

View product stock quantities across all companies directly from the product form without switching between companies.

Overview

Managing inventory in a multi-company environment usually requires switching between companies just to check stock availability.

Multi-Company Stock Details solves this problem by adding a dedicated Stock Details tab inside the Product form.

Authorized users can instantly view stock information across all companies from a single screen.

Key Features

Inventory Visibility

  • View stock across all companies.
  • Total quantity for all companies.
  • Warehouse/Location details.
  • Product Variant support.

Security

  • Dedicated Security Group.
  • Only authorized users can access the tab.
  • No changes to existing workflows.
  • Safe for Multi-Company environments.

Stock Information

  • On Hand Quantity.
  • Available Quantity.
  • Company Name.
  • Location Name.

User Experience

  • Integrated into Product Form.
  • Clean and responsive table.
  • Easy to use.
  • No configuration required.

Stock Details Tab

Displayed Information

Field Description
Total Qty (All Companies) Total inventory quantity across all companies.
Company Company owning the inventory.
Location Warehouse location.
On Hand Physical stock quantity.
Available Available quantity after reservations.
